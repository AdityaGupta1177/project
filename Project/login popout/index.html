Small Scale Farmer

Manages small farms, typically less than 2 hectares, focusing on local markets.

Medium Scale Farmer

Operates between 2-10 hectares, often using semi-modern equipment for production.

Large Scale Farmer

Runs farms over 10 hectares, utilizing advanced machinery and large distribution networks.

Wholesaler

Purchase crops in bulk directly from farmers and supply to retailers or markets at large scale.

Retailer

Buy products in smaller quantities and sell them directly to consumers through shops or stalls.

Distributor

Procure raw materials like grains, fruits, and vegetables to create packaged or processed food products.
